VitaSync: Health & BP Tracker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026. Developer: Misuhrobir (muhammadgarana@gmail.com). App ID: com.misuhrobir.vitasync.

Summary

VitaSync is offline-first. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no internet permission. All health data stays on your device. We never collect, sell, share or transfer your data to us or to any third party.

Data the app processes (on your device only)

  • Health and fitness data: blood pressure, heart rate, steps, distance, calories, sleep, weight and body composition, height, blood glucose, hydration and exercise sessions that you enter manually, read from Bluetooth health devices, or read from Health Connect with your permission.
  • Location: precise location is used only during a workout you start, to measure route distance and pace. It is stored on the device with that workout.
  • Physical activity: activity recognition is used to count steps and detect workouts.
  • Bluetooth: used only to connect to health devices you choose (heart-rate straps, scales, BP cuffs, oximeters, thermometers, glucometers). Bluetooth scanning is not used to determine location.

Health Connect

VitaSync reads and writes data in Health Connect only for the data types you allow, and only to show, sync and chart your own health data. Data from Health Connect is not sent off the device, is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to determine credit-worthiness or for lending. Use of data received from Health Connect follows the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements. You can revoke access any time in Health Connect settings.

Storage, security and deletion

Data is stored in the app's private storage on your phone and can be protected with biometric lock. You can export your data (PDF/CSV) yourself; files you export are shared only where you choose. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes everything it stored. Data in Health Connect stays under your control in Health Connect.

Children

VitaSync is not directed at children under 13.

Medical disclaimer

VitaSync is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition.
