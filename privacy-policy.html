Hidayah Privacy Policy

App: Hidayah (package com.aistudio.deencompanion.quranj) · Effective date: 10 October 2026

Hidayah is a Quran, prayer-times and Islamic learning app. It works mostly offline, shows no ads and never sells your data. Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Performance Monitoring help us understand app usage, crashes and performance. Collection starts automatically when you use the app, without an in-app permission prompt. Settings & Privacy describes the data collected.

Usage analytics

When you use the app, Google receives screen views and time spent, interaction identifiers, search usage (without query text), scroll milestones, audio actions and playback states, download outcomes and duration, permission outcomes, setting changes and network status and latency. Firebase also processes an app-instance identifier and standard app/device information; Google may derive approximate geographic information from network connections. These events can indicate which app features you use.

The events also include:

  • Which built-in content you open or play, by its fixed identifier in the app: for example the surah or para number, the Quran edition, the book collection, the naat reciter, the azan recording or the dua category. Books from the open World Library are reported only as “World Library”.
  • The setting options you choose: app language, prayer calculation method, Asr method, high-latitude rule, Hijri calendar, madhhab, Quran reader mode, whether prayer alerts and an azan alert are on, whether the home-screen widget is added, and whether your location comes from GPS or a city you picked. These are also kept as analytics user properties so reports can be grouped by them.
  • Feature milestones, without counts or details: for example that a Quran khatm, a Hifz Coach session, a tasbeeh round, a dhikr, a quiz round or a zakat calculation was completed, or that a masjid timing, a vote or a City Challenge join was saved.
  • Prayer alerts and widget: that an alert for a named prayer was delivered, opened or stopped, and whether the app was opened from the launcher, an alert or the widget.
  • Reading sessions: how long a reader was open and how many page turns it had, not which pages.

Our custom events do not include names, email addresses, phone numbers, your city, exact GPS coordinates, masjid names, search text, URLs, recordings, which prayers you marked or their status, tasbeeh or dhikr counts, the Salah guide’s man/woman choice, Muslimah-mode values, notes, financial amounts, whether zakat is due, or authentication IDs. Advertising ID collection and advertising personalization are disabled. We do not link analytics to your Firebase community account.

Crash reports and performance

Firebase Crashlytics sends a report when the app crashes or stops responding: the stack trace, app version, device model, Android version, free memory and storage, an installation identifier created by Crashlytics, and the last screen names and interaction identifiers described above. Firebase Performance Monitoring sends app start time, screen rendering (slow and frozen frames) and content download duration, with the same kind of device and app information and an approximate country. Neither includes your content, location or search text.

The app does not provide an Analytics collection switch. Uninstalling the app stops further app collection but does not erase events already received by Google. Contact us for deletion requests. See Firebase privacy information and Google's privacy policy.

1. Location

  • Prayer times, Qibla and Ramzan timetable: these are calculated on your device from your location. The coordinates stay on your phone.
  • Nearby masjids: when you open Community Masjid, your approximate coordinates are sent to public OpenStreetMap Overpass servers (overpass-api.de and mirrors) to find mosques near you.
  • Community votes and contributions: when you add a masjid, add a timing or vote, your current location goes to our Firebase backend. The server checks that you are within 200 m of the masjid. Your raw location is only used for this check. The server does not keep it with your vote.
  • Location permission is optional. You can also pick a city by hand.

2. Microphone

Hifz Coach uses your device's speech recognizer (usually Google) to check your recitation, and only while you press the mic button. We do not record, store or upload your audio. Your device's speech service handles recognition under its own privacy terms.

3. Community features (Firebase)

  • Community Masjid and City Challenge use Firebase Anonymous Authentication. This gives the app a random ID with no name, email or phone number.
  • We store in Google Cloud Firestore: masjids and timings you add, your votes, any content reports you send, and your City Challenge participation. Other users can see what you contribute. Your anonymous ID is never shown to them.
  • Firebase App Check uses Google Play Integrity to confirm that requests come from the real app. This helps stop abuse.
  • Google processes this data as our service provider: Firebase privacy.

4. Downloads from third-party hosts

Some content is downloaded when you ask for it. Your device connects directly to these hosts, and they can see your IP address as with any web request:

  • GitHub (github.com, raw.githubusercontent.com): Quran PDFs, word-by-word audio, hadith PDFs, naats
  • Quran.com / QuranCDN, EveryAyah, QuranicAudio, AlQuran Cloud: Quran data and recitation audio
  • Dawat-e-Islami (dawateislami.net), Internet Archive (archive.org), Google Drive, Emaan Library: books and hadith PDFs
  • jsDelivr CDN and currency-api.pages.dev: gold, silver and currency rates for the Zakat calculator

5. Data stored on your device

Your settings, bookmarks, reading progress, prayer tracking and downloaded files stay on your device. If you enable Android backup, they are included in your personal Google backup. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

6. Notifications and alarms

The app schedules prayer alerts on your device. The alert times and your city are not sent to us; usage analytics only records that an alert was delivered, opened or stopped, as described above.

7. Deleting your data

  • In the app, open Community Masjid → menu → "Delete my community data". This removes your votes and your user record, then deletes your anonymous account.
  • If you contributed a masjid or a timing, it can stay visible to others after deletion, but it is no longer linked to you.
  • You can also email us to ask for deletion. We handle requests within 30 days.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

9. Security

All network traffic uses HTTPS. Firestore security rules only allow users to change their own votes.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
